ME-Receipt
**Verified Digital Receipt & Tipping Tool on the ME Network**

🔗 **Live Mobile Demo:**  Demo:** https://ericko3414.github.io/ME-RECEIPT-/

## What It Does
ME-Receipt lets everyday vendors, freelancers, and clients send a payment, add an optional tip, and lock a permanent, verifiable digital receipt directly on the ME Network—all from a smartphone.

## The Problem It Solves
Small businesses and independent workers often lose paper receipts or rely on easily faked payment screenshots. ME-Receipt solves this by attaching an itemized, tamper-proof receipt directly to each payment.

## How It Works
1. **Enter Payment & Tip:** Input the vendor name, item or service description, amount (USDT or MEC), and an optional tip percentage.
2. **Pay & Lock Receipt:** Confirm the transaction with ultra-low ME Network gas fees.
3. **Instant Verification:** Generates a clean digital receipt card with a unique Receipt ID, timestamp, and payment breakdown.
